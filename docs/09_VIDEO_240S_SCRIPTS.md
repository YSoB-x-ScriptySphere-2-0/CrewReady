**Structure:** NASA Space Apps "240 Seconds of Glory" — WHO (45s) · WHY (60s) · WHAT (60s) · SO WHAT NEXT (75s)

**Rule §3.3:** No recognisable name, voice or face of anyone under 18 in the video.

## 3. Reading Notes (Narrator)

- Read aloud before recording. Change any word that doesn't feel natural to you. The meaning must stay.
- **Pause** after `"That's CrewReady."` and after `"the game remembered."`
- Say `"DONKI"` as `"DON-key"`. Say `"NOAA"` as `"NO-ah"`. Do not spell out APIs.
- Keep shot 1D warm and genuine — not scripted-sounding.
- If the read runs over 230 s: trim Shot 2D to one sentence and Shot 4B to three sentences.
- **Never mention any child's real name or describe their appearance.**
