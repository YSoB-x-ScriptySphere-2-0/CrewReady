# CrewReady: 240-Second Voiceover Script

Voiceover lines only, with timestamps. Pace is about 2.2 words per second, so read it lively but not rushed. `[ ]` boxes are blanks to fill in before recording. `(beat)` is a one-second pause.

---

## One-line solution (for the submission form)

CrewReady is a space survival game where kids become junior astronauts, make smart choices with limited resources, and learn real NASA science while finding humanity's new home.

## Summary (for the submission form)

Space games are either too hard or too simple, so kids never get the real science with the fun. CrewReady fixes that. Kids play as Orbit or Nova, guided by Twinkle, a sidekick who speaks Gen Alpha. Across six levels built on NASA data, every choice costs resources, and the storm size and runner speed come from today's real space weather. It comes with the micro-games kids already love, and it works in English and Bangla.

## Best features (the hook highlights)

1. Storm size and runner speed set by today's real NASA and NOAA space weather
2. Twinkle, a guide who knows Gen Alpha slang
3. Learning by doing: every decision costs something
4. Micro-games kids already play, to keep attention
5. Bilingual in Bangla and English, for local impact

## THE SCRIPT

### 0:00 – 0:15 | THE HOOK

> Your decision can keep humanity alive. Are you ready?
> (beat) No worries, dude. CrewReady will make you READY!
> A space survival game where kids become Orbit or Nova, team up with Twinkle, and race to find humanity's new home. Powered by real NASA data!

### 0:15 – 0:40 | WHAT MAKES IT SPECIAL

> Why kids won't put it down:
> The storm is sized from real NASA space weather, because space has no "easy mode."
> Twinkle speaks fluent Gen Alpha, so learning never sounds like homework.
> Every choice costs something, so kids learn by deciding.
> Mini-games they already love keep them focused.
> And it speaks Bangla and English.

### 0:40 – 1:10 | WHO & WHY

> Hi! We are [Team Name], Gen Z-ers with different skills and one obsession: the universe.
> It began with "Twinkle, twinkle, little star," then grew with Kazi Nazrul Islam's Shonkolpo.
> *(Bangla)* "থাকব নাকো বদ্ধ ঘরে, দেখব এবার জগৎটাকে..."
> Kids have that same spark, but an attention span shorter than a shooting star. And space games are either real rocket science, or no science at all.
> So we built CrewReady, so their curiosity never gets ghosted.

### 1:10 – 1:55 | WHAT: THE MISSION

> Earth is in trouble. Pollution, pandemics, running out of resources... or maybe the robots get ideas. (beat) Kidding. Mostly.
> So you take command as Orbit or Nova, with Twinkle beside you, to find a new shelter for humankind.
> Every choice costs power, air, water or food. Choose wisely and the crew survives. Run out, and humanity's future becomes one big question mark.
> Six levels, all built on NASA data. You pack the rocket, because space has no grocery store. You dodge solar storms, land after a seven-minute delay, survive a dust storm, and grow food in space. Finally, you find your crewmates, because nobody saves humanity alone.

### 1:55 – 2:20 | THE CREW

> Speaking of crewmates, meet ours!
> The Team Lead studied what keeps young gamers hooked. The Researcher dug through NASA data, so every fact is real. The Back-End Developer led development and built the engine. The Front-End Developer built the front end, the UI/UX Designer designed the look and feel, and the Video Editor wrote the script, yes, including this joke.
> And all of us made this video together.

### 2:20 – 2:55 | FEATURES & EVIDENCE

> Bonus mini-games they already know earn stardust, which powers a real in-game economy.
> There's Fly, an endless run through space obstacles. A Resources tab to stretch what you have. A Leaderboard for friendly rivalry. A Profile that tracks every mission. All with playful music, in the language you choose.
> And it's real: our levels use NASA DONKI for solar storms, NOAA for space weather, NeoWs for asteroids, and OSDR for plants in space.
> [EVIDENCE: "When [X] kids playtested it, [result or quote]."]

### 2:55 – 3:15 | REAL-LIFE IMPACT

> Behind the fun are real skills: deciding under pressure, planning ahead, and managing limited resources. That's pocket-money management in a space suit.
> Kids learn real science, practice teamwork, and keep asking, "What's out there?"

### 3:15 – 3:45 | THE VISION

> And this is just liftoff.
> A Training tab with focus and memory games like astronauts do, plus physical simulations we'd love to build with NASA.
> A Reels tab to scroll amazing space facts, make videos, and build a creator community.
> After Level 6, multiplayer rooms, where friends join from different devices, each with a role.
> More worlds, starting with Mars. And more languages after Bangla.

### 3:45 – 4:00 | CLOSING

> Our mission is simple: spark the curiosity, then give it a place to grow, the way the stars were part of our childhood.

> So... are you ready? (beat) No worries. We are. Thank you!
