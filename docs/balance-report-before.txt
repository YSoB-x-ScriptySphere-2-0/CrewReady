CrewReady balance check (BUILD_SEQUENCE F3)

The mission has about 10^19 paths, too many to list, so this plays random missions: every step picks any of its
moves with equal chance (200,000 missions for each of the 9 storm and dust cases), and separately searches every
path for a winning one. The real engine and the real levels are used. A mission is WON when all six levels are
finished with no meter at 0. Target: 20–60% win, no option in over 70% of wins, a careful player can win level 6.
